📂 Upload your model

Your model is loaded locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to a server.

⚙️ Model settings

The scale slider changes the size of your model. In VR, the model is positioned approximately one metre to your right, relative to the starting view.

ℹ️ How to use

1. Select a GLB file.
2. Rotate the preview by dragging with your mouse or finger.
3. Adjust the model scale if needed.
4. Press Enter VR on a compatible headset and browser.
5. Look around to see the model beside you.

VR requires HTTPS and a browser/device supporting WebXR immersive-vr sessions. GitHub Pages provides HTTPS. Ordinary mobile browsers may not support immersive VR.
